Distinguishing Root Rot from Under-Watering in Terracotta

Terracotta wicks exterior moisture quickly. Under-watered foliage shows uniform limpness with crisp, pale tips; anaerobic root rot exhibits soft, mushy black petioles accompanied by dark sulfurous odor at base drainage.

Edema vs. Scale Infestation: Identifying Under-Leaf Bumps

Edema bumps are physiological cellular ruptures firmly bonded to leaf veins that do not scrape off without tearing tissue. Scale insects (Coccidae) are separate organisms easily dislodged with an alcohol-soaked swab.

Winter Dormancy Acclimation vs. Light Starvation

Winter dormancy involves slowed apical growth while leaf color remains dense deep green. Light starvation triggers severe etiolar elongation (long thin stems stretching toward windows) with yellowing lower fronds.
