## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the calm, grounded elegance of a high-end botanical atelier. It bridges the tactile, organic richness of conservatory living with the crisp precision of a contemporary luxury publication. The target audience seeks curated greenery, artisanal planters, and horticultural guidance, valuing provenance, sustainability, and refined aesthetics over mass-market commercialism.

The design movement synthesizes **Editorial Minimalism** with **Warm Tactility**:
- Generous cream-toned negative space establishes an unhurried, gallery-like browsing experience.
- High-contrast, graceful serif headlines convey heritage, botanical knowledge, and craftsmanship.
- Clean, open humanist sans-serif body typography guarantees immediate legibility and digital fluidity.
- Subtle physical details—softly grounded elevations, organic border transitions, and restrained terracotta punctuation—lend a natural, physical presence without resorting to heavy skeuomorphism.

## Colors

The palette is derived from conservatory flora, aged terra sigillata, and warm linen paper.

- **Primary (`#1B4D3E` — Deep Botanical Emerald):** Anchors core brand touchpoints, primary conversion actions, high-contrast typography, and critical state indicators. Evokes lush, shaded foliage.
- **Secondary (`#556B2F` — Sunlit Olive):** Serves as an organic supporting tone for badges, subtle accent strokes, secondary tags, and botanical categorization (e.g., care difficulty, light tiers).
- **Tertiary (`#C86D51` — Warm Terracotta):** Used sparingly to denote active notifications, promotional callouts, stock urgency, and selective hover micro-interactions. Draws inspiration from unglazed earthen pottery.
- **Neutral Surface (`#F9F8F3` — Organic Cream):** Replaces harsh stark whites across primary canvas backgrounds, softening contrast to simulate natural unbleached stock.
- **Supporting Neutral Text (`#2C3531` — Deep Forest Peat):** Provides high-contrast, softer-than-pure-black reading comfort for continuous editorial copy.
- **Surface Elevation Variants:** `#F2EFE9` for secondary card backdrops and soft inset containers; `#E5DFD5` for muted divider rules and borders.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances the stately, literary cadence of `Playfair Display` with the crystal-clear, approachable warmth of `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

- **Editorial Titles & Product Names (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for hero banners, collection headers, narrative callouts, and botanical species titles. It evokes botanical encyclopedias and luxury magazine spreads. It should always be set with tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) to maintain optical density.
- **Product Descriptions & Operational Copy (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Powers product specs, botanical care requirements, prices, long-form stories, checkout paths, and interface controls. Its generous counters and modern geometry offer high legibility on small viewports and retain warmth against cream backgrounds.
- **Uppercase Labels:** Specialized badges (e.g., "PET FRIENDLY", "RARE FIND", "LOW LIGHT") use `label-sm` set in uppercase with a slight positive letter spacing (`0.04em`) to establish visual structure without adding visual noise.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model utilizes a structured 12-column responsive grid paired with an 8-point vertical cadence to maintain editorial pacing and ample breathing room.

- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12-column fluid grid, maximum content width constrained to `1320px`, `3rem` (`48px`) outer canvas margins, and `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters. Editorial feature blocks can alternate between asymmetric 7/5 column pairings to showcase plant photography alongside narrative care details.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout, `2rem` (`32px`) outer margins, `1.5rem` gutters. Multi-column catalog layouts collapse to 2 or 3 items across.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `1.25rem` (`20px`) margins, `1rem` (`16px`) gutters. Grid rows collapse into single-column or dual-card carousels with horizontal swipe gestures for collection views.
- **Rhythm & White Space:** Spacing scales should be used generously around hero features and photography. Group related items (such as a plant thumbnail, Latin name, and price) using `space-xs` and `space-sm`, reserving `space-xl` for section separation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is achieved through **Tonal Layering** coupled with **Warm Organic Ambient Shadows**. Hard drop shadows and glass blurs are avoided in order to preserve the tactile, physical presence of a boutique paper journal.

- **Base Canvas:** Neutral cream tone (`#F9F8F3`) provides the foundation.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Inset Surface):** Subtle tonal shift to `#F2EFE9` or pure `#FFFFFF` bounded by an ultra-soft hairline rule (`rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.08)`).
- **Layer 2 (Floating Overlays, Cart Drawers, Popovers):** Elevated with an ambient, diffused shadow tinted with warm peat and deep green:
  `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(27, 45, 38, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(27, 45, 38, 0.04);`
- **Interactive Depth:** Cards and actionable elements rely on subtle Y-axis elevation shifts (`-2px` translation) and smooth shadow expansion rather than harsh color changes on hover, maintaining a smooth, unhurried feel.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Rounded (`2`)** structural geometry. 

- Standard interactive components (buttons, text fields, chips, select menus) use a base radius of `0.5rem` (`8px`).
- Larger organizational cards, modal sheets, and featured imagery containers implement `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`).
- Oversized promotional containers, discount badges, and floating bottom sheets utilize `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`).
- Botanical imagery containers can optionally pair rounded corners with gentle organic arch masks for curated seasonal hero banners, nodding to classic conservatory architecture.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#1B4D3E` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` border radius, `0.875rem` vertical and `1.75rem` horizontal padding. Hover transforms with a slight brightness lift and soft ambient green shadow.
- **Secondary:** Transparent background, `1.5px` border in `#1B4D3E`, `#1B4D3E` text. Hover fills background with `rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.05)`.
- **Tertiary / Accent:** Solid `#C86D51` background with white text, reserved for "Add to Cart", flash promotions, or critical checkouts.
- **Text Link:** Underlined with a natural offset, transitioning from `#1B4D3E` to `#556B2F` on hover.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Filter Chips:** Height of `36px`, rounded `0.5rem` radius, `#F2EFE9` background, `#2C3531` text with `1px` border in `#E5DFD5`. 
- **Selected State:** `#1B4D3E` background with white text and no border.
- **Care Metric Chips:** Compact tags indicating "Low Light", "Weekly Water", or "Pet Safe" using a soft `#556B2F` tint (`10%` fill) with `#556B2F` text and botanical line iconography.

### Product & Editorial Cards
- **Product Card:** Elevated on a clean `#FFFFFF` or `#F2EFE9` surface with `1rem` corner rounding. Image aspect ratio set to `4:5` with warm desaturated background staging. Botanical name in `headline-sm` Playfair Display, price in `title-md` Plus Jakarta Sans.
- **Editorial Banner Card:** Full-bleed natural photography overlaid with warm `#F9F8F3` container boxes holding serif editorial quotes, story snippets, and collection links.

### Input Fields & Selects
- **Text Inputs:** Height of `48px`, `0.5rem` border radius, background in `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E5DFD5`. Placeholder text in muted peat (`#7A827D`).
- **Focus State:** Border changes to `1.5px solid #1B4D3E` with an ambient ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.12)`. No harsh primary outlines.
- **Select Drawers:** Custom drop-downs featuring clean chevron indicators and warm cream dropdown menus with `1rem` rounding.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Selection Controls:** `20px` custom controls with `0.25rem` radius for checkboxes and circular for radios. Inactive border of `1.5px solid #B7B0A2`. Active state fills with `#1B4D3E` displaying a crisp white check or center pip.

### Botanical Care Gauges & Plant Meters
- Specialized components visualizing light, humidity, and water requirements. Segmented micro-bars with `0.25rem` radius filled in Sunlit Olive (`#556B2F`) over a muted `#E5DFD5` track, accompanied by concise textual descriptors.
