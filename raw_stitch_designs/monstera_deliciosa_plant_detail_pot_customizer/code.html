The 12-Month Living Protocol

Monstera Deliciosa responds dramatically to indoor seasonal transitions. Follow our head horticulturist's rhythm to maximize deep fenestration and prevent root fatigue.

01 Active Growth Phase • March to May

Awaken with Diluted Organic Feed & Foliage Rotation

As daylight expands, your Monstera initiates new aerial nodes and emergent rolled fronds. Feed every 14 days with half-strength seaweed mycorrhizae elixir. Rotate the vessel 90 degrees clockwise every week toward indirect light to maintain balanced symmetric leaf expansion.
